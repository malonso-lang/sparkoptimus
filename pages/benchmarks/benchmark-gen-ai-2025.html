What's inside

The 2025 edition benchmarks how organizations across sectors adopted generative AI: which use cases made it past the pilot stage, how maturity differs by industry, and what value leaders were able to report.

It also sets out the practical blockers that came up most often — unclear ownership, fragmented data, and capability gaps — and how the front-runners dealt with them.

Use it to place your own organization against peers and to decide where the next investment should go.
