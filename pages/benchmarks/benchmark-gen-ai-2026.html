What's inside

The 2026 edition tracks how organizations have moved from generative AI experiments to agentic systems that carry real work. We benchmark adoption, maturity and realised value across sectors, and compare the leaders with the majority still stuck in pilots.

The picture is uneven. A small group has industrialised AI — clear ownership, reusable data foundations, and agents embedded in day-to-day processes. Most organizations are still running disconnected use cases with limited measurable impact.

The benchmark shows what separates the two groups, and what to fix first if you want AI to compound rather than accumulate.
