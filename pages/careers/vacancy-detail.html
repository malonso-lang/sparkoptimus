About the role

We're looking for an experienced Senior Digital Strategist to join our Amsterdam office and lead transformative projects for Europe's most ambitious organizations. In this role, you'll combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution, working across technology, organizational change, and business model innovation. Your expertise will directly impact how leading brands navigate digital disruption.
