Description

Are you looking for a role where you'll be supported and encouraged to grow at a rapid pace? Do you want to explore how AI, digital, and sustainability transform companies across all industries? Do you want to build skills in developing and implementing practical strategies that shape the future of how we do business? Then SparkOptimus could be the right place for you.

What you'll do

As an Associate, you'll have the opportunity to make an impact from day one, in a supportive, team-based environment where no two days are the same. You won't just learn every skill, not only supporting and performing analyses, but also collaborating closely on real-world transformation. You won't be expected to know everything upfront – we value curiosity, teamwork, and a willingness to learn. These experiences, combined with ongoing mentorship from Managers, Partners, and clients, offer a wide range of learning opportunities. SparkOptimus has a strong coaching and learning culture. We support your development through on-the-job coaching and structured training programs focused on challenges you may encounter as an Associate – including consulting skills, interpersonal development, and digital expertise. We recognise that growth is personal and non-linear, and we're committed to helping you thrive in your own way.

Why SparkOptimus?

SparkOptimus helps organizations unlock the power of disruption. We spark and scale transformational initiatives that improve the lives of consumers and environments in sustainable ways. We pride ourselves on our thought leadership and solutions that drive transformational change for organisations across the globe for organizations like Heineken, FrieslandCampina, Edens, ING, IKEA, and AlisoNikel. We work with leaders to clarify what needs to be done and how to do it. Together, we build and embed the capabilities and culture needed to make change happen at every level of the organization. At SparkOptimus, we believe that diversity and inclusion are essential to who we are and how we work. We offer equal opportunities and fair treatment for all current and future employees, and we strive to ensure everyone feels seen, valued, and supported. Moreover, SparkOptimus is a certified B Corp, meaning we meet high standards of social and environmental impact, transparency, and accountability. We are committed to using business as a force for good–driving positive change for our clients, employees, and society. Join us in making a meaningful impact while shaping the future of digital transformation.

Benefits

On top of that and our primary compensation package, we offer an attractive package of secondary benefits, such as:

  • Flexible time off: buy additional holidays, go on sabbatical or work remotely up to one month a year
  • Culture & fun: regular company events and trips (including our annual SparkWeekend), Friday drinks, office messages, milestone gifts, and more!

Requirements

  • Recent graduates of a master's program
  • Strong academic track record. Dutch students must have completed their VWO
  • Experience and/or interest in business, operations, start-ups, e-commerce, innovation, IT, AI, or sustainability is a plus
  • Analytical thinking, strong interpersonal skills, and a collaborative mindset
  • A proactive mindset, willingness to learn, and a passion for making a difference / change
  • We welcome applicants from all backgrounds. If you're unsure whether you're a fit, we encourage you to apply

Please note: We do not offer visa sponsorships, so if you now or in the future will need visa sponsorship, we unfortunately will not be able to move forward with your application. Thank you for understanding.
