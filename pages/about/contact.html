What this means

End-to-end transformation, now with global reach

Since we founded SparkOptimus 15 years ago, we've grown into a leading Dutch AI and digital transformation consultancy, helping ambitious organizations turn technological change into measurable business impact.

Joining EY-Parthenon marks an exciting next chapter for us. SparkOptimus brings deep expertise in AI, digital strategy and hands-on execution. EY-Parthenon adds complementary strategy and transformation capabilities, plus the reach of a global organization. Together, the teams can support clients from strategic direction through to large-scale execution.

For our clients, that means the same team, the same pragmatic way of working, but now with more reach behind it. That translates to new opportunities and more impact we can make together.
